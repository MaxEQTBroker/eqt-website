**Headline:** EQT Real Estate Expands Multilingual Advisory for International Buyers on Palm Jumeirah

**Subhead:** Boutique Dubai brokerage offers English, Russian and Ukrainian guidance across the emirate's prime freehold communities

**Summary (for newswire submission field):** EQT Real Estate, a boutique Dubai brokerage, highlights growing international demand for Palm Jumeirah and offers multilingual advisory in English, Russian and Ukrainian across the emirate's prime freehold communities.

**Dateline:** Dubai, United Arab Emirates, [16 September 2026]

---

EQT Real Estate, a boutique Dubai brokerage specialising in luxury and prime property, today highlighted growing international interest in Palm Jumeirah and Dubai's prime freehold communities, and reaffirmed its multilingual advisory service for buyers from Europe, the United Kingdom and the Commonwealth of Independent States.

Dubai allows foreign nationals to own property outright in designated freehold areas, with title registered at the Dubai Land Department and no annual property tax on the home. A property purchase from AED 2 million can also qualify the buyer for a 10 year Golden Visa, combining a prime asset with long term residency. These conditions continue to draw international buyers to landmark addresses such as Palm Jumeirah.

"Buyers arriving from the UK, Europe and the CIS want clear, honest guidance in their own language, and they want a specialist who knows Palm Jumeirah frond by frond," said Max Karnaukh, Chief Technology Officer at EQT Real Estate. "Our focus is narrow by design. We are a boutique team, we work in English, Russian and Ukrainian, and we concentrate on the prime communities our clients actually ask for."

EQT publishes trilingual community guides and answer-first market resources across its website at https://eqt.ae, covering Palm Jumeirah, Emirates Hills, Dubai Hills Estate and other prime communities, alongside guidance on freehold ownership, off plan purchases and the Golden Visa.

**About EQT Real Estate**
EQT Real Estate (EQT Real Estate Brokerage LLC) is a boutique Dubai brokerage specialising in luxury and prime property, including Palm Jumeirah. The firm serves international buyers and investors in English, Russian and Ukrainian, and is licensed in Dubai (RERA ORN 33039). More at https://eqt.ae.
